Stove Remote puts your MCZ Maestro pellet stove on your wrist. It talks to the stove directly over Bluetooth: no phone needed, no cloud account, no internet connection.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• See the room temperature, the setpoint and what the stove is doing (off, cleaning, loading, ignition, stabilization, on, turning off)
• Turn the stove on or off, always behind a confirmation screen
• Change the setpoint in 0.5 °C steps
• Pick the mode: Manual, Auto, Overnight, Comfort or Turbo
• Set the power level (1 to 5) and the fan (1 to 5 or Auto)
• See active alarms
• Add the Stove tile to check the temperature with one swipe and turn the stove on or off

BUILT FOR SAFETY
• Every on/off goes through a confirmation screen
• The stove state is read again right before switching it on or off, so a stale screen can never switch it the wrong way
• The app refuses to switch while the stove is shutting down, and waits 60 seconds between two switches
• The app never sends a command on its own: opening the app or the tile only reads values
• The age of the displayed values is always shown

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Works fully offline over Bluetooth Low Energy
• No account, no ads, no analytics, no data collection
• The watch releases the Bluetooth link after 30 seconds so the official app can still connect

REQUIREMENTS
• Wear OS 4 or newer (Pixel Watch, Galaxy Watch 4 and later, and others)
• An MCZ stove with the Maestro Bluetooth module

FIRST PAIRING
On the stove panel, press + and − together until the Bluetooth icon blinks, then pick your stove in the app. Pairing only works while the icon blinks.

OPEN SOURCE
Stove Remote is open source (Apache 2.0): https://github.com/nosari20/MCZ-WearOS
